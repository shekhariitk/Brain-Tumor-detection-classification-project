About Brain Tumor Detection

How It Works

Our brain tumor detection system uses a deep convolutional neural network trained on thousands of MRI scans to identify potential tumors. The model analyzes patterns in the MRI images that are characteristic of different types of brain tumors.

Disclaimer

This system is designed to assist healthcare professionals and should not be used as a sole diagnostic tool. Always consult with a qualified neurologist or radiologist for diagnosis.
